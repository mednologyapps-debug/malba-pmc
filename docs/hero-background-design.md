## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide photographic background for MALBA PMC project management homepage hero, behind white text on left and an existing separate 3D electrical tower illustration on right. Primary request: subtle architectural image adding depth without clutter. Scene: close-up of a contemporary engineering building facade with only a few broad smooth concrete planes and very restrained vertical seams, gently angled perspective, quiet monumental architecture. Composition: wide landscape 3:2, generous calm empty surfaces across entire frame, no dominant focal object. Color palette: monochromatic muted blue #073F7C and cool silver-blue, soft even lighting with faint material texture. Professional editorial architectural photography, realistic high definition. Avoid: electrical towers, pylons, cables, substation equipment, people, skylines, signage, text, logos, diagrams, grids, busy windows, decorative gradients, bright highlights, purple or warm colors. Must remain a subtle background, never a second foreground object.
